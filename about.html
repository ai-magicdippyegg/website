Our origin

How it all began

Magic Dippy Egg was born from a simple breakfast-table observation: the most satisfying things in life are soft on the inside, perfectly timed, and best enjoyed with a little dipping. We thought — why can't AI feel the same way?

So we cracked open the idea in early 2025, whisked together a small team of engineers, designers, and one very opinionated breakfast enthusiast, and set about building something warm, intelligent, and genuinely delightful.

Today, Magic Dippy Egg is the AI companion that greets you gently, answers you honestly, and never, ever overcooks.
