Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the Magic Dippy Egg. If your question isn't here, feel free to reach out to our flock!

What exactly is a Magic Dippy Egg?

It's a whimsical AI companion designed to bring warmth and delight to your digital life. Think of it as a perfectly soft-boiled egg filled with intelligence instead of yolk (though we love yolk too!).

Is it a real egg?

While we draw inspiration from the culinary perfection of a dippy egg, our product is 100% digital and 0% cholesterol. No chickens were involved in the making of this AI.

How do I "dip" into the AI?

Dipping is our way of saying "interacting." You can ask questions, request creative help, or just have a chat. Each interaction is like dipping a piece of toast into a warm, runny yolk.

Is my data safe in the shell?

Absolutely. We take security very seriously. Your data is protected by our "Shell-Tight" encryption, ensuring that what happens inside the egg stays inside the egg.

Does it cost anything?

We offer a free tier that's perfect for casual dippers. For those who need more frequent dips and advanced features, we have a "Golden Yolk" premium plan.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes! Magic Dippy Egg is fully responsive and works beautifully on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. You can take your egg to go!

Our team is always ready to help you crack any problem.
